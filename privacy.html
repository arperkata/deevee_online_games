The short version

Brain Games Arcade is made for everyone, including children. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no chat. We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, photos, locations or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

The games save a few things in your browser's local storage so that the games work the way you left them:

  • your best scores, stars and chapter progress;
  • settings such as sound, voice, hints and the on-screen keyboard;
  • player names you type for 2-player games (so you do not have to type them again).

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can erase it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Sound and voice

Voice prompts use your browser's built-in text-to-speech to speak. The games never use your microphone or camera.

Other websites that load with the games

  • Google Fonts (the rounded letters) and cdnjs.cloudflare.com (the free three.js 3D library used by the dragon games) may receive your device's IP address when the page loads, as every website does. We do not combine this with anything else.
  • Ads (only if switched on). If advertising is enabled, it is configured as child-directed with non-personalised ads only: no interest-based advertising, no remarketing and no behavioural profiles. Ad providers may still use limited technical data to show and measure ads, as described in their own policies.

Play-money games

The slot-style and "luck" games use pretend coins only. There is no real money, no purchases, no prizes and no way to cash out. They are in a separate section and are intended for ages 13 and up.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Parents and teachers can use the games freely. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.
